Common questions

A few useful answers.

What can BQ Technologies build?+

We build custom web and mobile applications, AI-assisted workflows, business systems and cloud solutions. We also assess and strengthen the security of digital products and infrastructure.

Do you work with existing systems?+

Yes. We can improve an existing product, connect systems through APIs, modernise older workflows or plan a careful migration where replacement is the better option.

How does a project begin?+

It begins with a focused conversation about your organisation, the problem and the desired outcome. We then recommend a practical next step and define scope before development starts.

Can you support non-technical teams?+

Absolutely. We explain decisions in plain language, involve the right people at each stage and prepare users for the new system as part of delivery.

Do you provide ongoing support?+

Support and maintenance can be included based on the needs of the system. The exact arrangement is agreed clearly as part of the project scope.

Common questions

Before we get started.

Have another question? Send us a message and we will help.

What can BQ Technologies build?+

We build web and mobile applications, AI assisted workflows, business systems and cloud solutions. We also assess and strengthen the security of digital products and infrastructure.

Do you work with existing systems?+

Yes. We can improve an existing product, connect systems through APIs, modernise older workflows or plan a careful migration.

How does a project begin?+

It begins with a focused conversation about your organisation, the problem and the outcome you need. We then recommend a practical next step and define scope before development starts.

Can you support nontechnical teams?+

Yes. We explain decisions in plain language, involve the right people and help users prepare for the new system.

Do you provide ongoing support?+

Support and maintenance can be included based on the needs of the system. The exact arrangement is agreed as part of the project scope.

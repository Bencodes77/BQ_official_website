Privacy Policy

How we handle information when you use our website or contact us.

Last updated: 3 August 2026

1. Introduction

BQ Technologies respects your privacy. This policy explains what information may reach us when you use bqtech.co.tz, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

2. Information we receive

This website does not create user accounts, run analytics, place marketing cookies, or store contact form submissions. If you choose to contact us through email, phone, WhatsApp, or social media, we receive the information you send through that service, which may include your name, contact details, organisation, and project information.

3. The contact form

The contact form on this website prepares an email in your device’s email application. The form content is not submitted to or stored by the website. Your email provider processes the message when you choose to send it.

4. How we use information

  • To respond to enquiries and discuss potential work.
  • To provide services where we enter into a client agreement.
  • To maintain business records and meet legal obligations.
  • To protect our services, clients, and organisation from misuse.

5. Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with service providers where needed to communicate or deliver agreed services, or where disclosure is required by law. We retain business communications only for as long as reasonably needed for the purpose for which they were received.

6. External services

Links to WhatsApp and social platforms take you to third party services with their own privacy practices. We encourage you to review their policies before sharing information.

7. Your choices

You may ask us to explain, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you, subject to any legal or contractual obligations that require us to retain it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our website or practices change. The date above shows the latest revision.
